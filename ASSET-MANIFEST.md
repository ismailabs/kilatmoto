# KILAT MOTORWORKS — asset provenance

All runtime imagery, fonts and SVGs are local. AI-generated imagery illustrates a fictional automotive concept; it is not evidence of real vehicles, staff, premises, treatments or outcomes.

## New identity and photography

The supplied 17 October 2026 handoff includes three original AI-generated scenes and local WebP derivatives: `hero-vehicle` (1600/960 desktop and 900 mobile), `paint-correction` (1200/720) and `wheel-detail` (1200/720). These are copied unchanged into `assets/photos/`; source hashes and dimensions are recorded in `kilatmoto-Codex-Handoff/data/asset-manifest.json`. All were visually inspected before use.

`assets/brand/` contains the handoff's original editable wordmarks, lightning symbol and precision grid. The live logo is selectable HTML text spelling KILAT MOTORWORKS. The favicon uses the supplied lightning symbol. Supporting `assets/icons/wordmark-*.svg` use the same new identity.

`assets/brand/social-overlay.svg` is an editable 1200×630 original composition with correct brand, headline and explicit fictional ISMAIL credit. `og-image.webp` combines this vector overlay with the supplied hero illustration. It is a social graphic, not a flattened webpage. The design boards and implementation-reference PNGs are never loaded as site UI.

The four boards in the handoff are inspiration only. Alternate KILAT MOTO text, statistics and claims are excluded. The precise Home/Services desktop/mobile mockups guide composition. Actual browser screenshots are independently captured under `qa-artifacts/`.

## Retained artwork and typography

The six `assets/images/project-*.webp` images preserve the supplied project identities. Their historical manifest records generated artwork and crops; original generation records are not independently reconstructed. `interior-care.webp` is retained from the previous build and depicts an unbranded illustrative cabin. The Family SUV image identifies the vehicle category; its interior-care story remains conceptual.

Unused historical source-package artwork remains in assets to preserve project history. It is not a claim of new work. `assets/fonts/space-grotesk.ttf` is the previously supplied Space Grotesk 700 font with its unchanged SIL Open Font License in `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`. Body text uses system Arial/Helvetica. No remote font or image request occurs during browsing.
