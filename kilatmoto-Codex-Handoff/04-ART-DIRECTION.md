## Decision rubric — measured qualitatively, no fake numerical scoring
1. **Brand correctness and truthfulness:** logo says `KILAT MOTORWORKS`; visitor can tell fictional studio from the outset; no fake shop contact or social proof.
2. **Commercial clarity:** services, projects and direct ISMAIL attribution are readily accessible; automotive owner understands why site quality matters.
3. **Responsive integrity:** homepage headline and CTA legible at 320–430px; service comparison supports keyboard and touch.
4. **Distinctiveness:** dramatic editorial photo and asymmetric layout, not a repetitive black-box grid.
5. **Accessibility and production cost:** high contrast, stable cropping, local image variants, meaningful interactions, reduced motion.

## Recommended synthesis
Use **primary board 01** as cinematic language; supplement its page modules with the precise, text-correct implementation reference mockups (see `design/implementation-mockups/`). Correct invented on-board phrases before implementing. Prioritise real content and audited components over purely decorative UI.

## Photo strategy
- Large low-angle unbranded vehicle photo in hero; enough dark negative space for left-side type on desktop; crop mobile around car and move headings onto gradient overlay.
- Service images depict paint correction, wheel/glass protection, premium automotive finish, and original source cards where useful. The provided photos are **fictional AI-generated illustrative visuals**, not documentary work evidence.
- Editorial intervals: exact panel spacing, photo overprint, thin bronze divider, structured light lime CTA, subdued decorative technical grid.

## Never import concept-board misinformation
AI concept compositions may depict real marque-like emblems, false claims, client stats, fictitious names or phone numbers. Don't use such details or treat mockup text as confirmed. Do not promise guaranteed outcomes or claim customers exist. The final website must be built from source code and confirmed functional contracts, not flattened screenshots.
