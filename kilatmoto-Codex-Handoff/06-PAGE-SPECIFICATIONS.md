# Five-page design-to-code specification

## `/` — Home
1. Slender top concept notice — **“Fictional automotive detailing concept by ISMAIL”** linked to creator context, not a dismissible legal afterthought.
2. Sticky but measured nav: KILAT MOTORWORKS, Home/About/Services/Projects/Contact; differentiated primary CTA to services and distinct ISMAIL link.
3. Hero: `Precision in every finish.` or similarly authentic sample copy (no false achievements), short clarifying paragraph, two CTAs **Explore services** and **View projects**, local cinematic vehicle image. Primary mockup demonstrates composition, not binding exact copy.
4. Compact value strip: clarity of care; protection; considered craftsmanship; responsible recommendations — do not make fake certification promises.
5. Six-service preview grouped by appropriate categories, with full list link, meaningful images and 1–2-sentence summaries.
6. Editorial half-photo process section (assess → recommend → detail → verify), explicitly presented as a hypothetical process.
7. Six-project conceptual showcase highlights, with “concept” chips, no fake before/after promises.
8. Creator/ISMAIL CTA with language for actual business-owner viewers: **“Imagining this experience for your automotive business? Explore websites by ISMAIL.”**
9. FAQ and footer, all page links valid.

## `/about/` — About
- 3-part brand story: precision, visible care, Malaysian driving context. Fictional workshop positioning made unambiguous.
- Craft/process modules using paint-correction photograph and editorial photography; distinguish *visual treatment concept* from completed jobs.
- Present principles and process, not invented founders, testimonials or named personnel.
- Bridge to Services and genuine ISMAIL creator CTA.

## `/services/` — Services
- Visually stronger service landing with six preserved offerings **exact labels** from contract.
- Cards alternate photo, inclusions/benefits, suitability, limitations, realistic non-absolute care advice.
- Preserve source service IDs (`exterior`, `interior`, `paint`, `ceramic`, `wheels`, `maintenance`) and contact demo selection/deep-link mapping.
- Avoid specific product ingredients, lifetime warranties, paint correction guarantees, accredited installer claims, pricing and real studio packages unless grounded in current source and approved.
- Optional accessible simple filter/tabs only if navigation remains obvious and source content readable without JS.

## `/projects/` — Projects
- Six fictional case studies preserved; provenance and conceptual nature explained near gallery.
- Accessible focus-filter buttons, result counts, and static fallback with all items visible.
- Photo viewer supports click/keyboard, previous/next where appropriate, Escape/focus restoration; linked originals when JS unavailable.
- Refrain from falsifying customer reactions and physical outcomes; call examples **visual concepts**.

## `/contact/` — Contact demo
- Clear conceptual location wording **“Kuala Lumpur / Klang Valley — fictional studio”**, no exact address, number, calendar or fake business email.
- Enquiry demonstration validates locally and states **“No enquiry was sent, submitted, booked or saved.”** This must remain true by inspection and network tests.
- Separate link invites real website-project enquiries via verified ISMAIL portfolio destination.
- No real data collection or personal-information storage without a separately approved backend/privacy plan.

## Cross-page navigation/footer
- All five pages feature consistent navigation and attribution. Any new split architecture must remain lightweight, maintainable and preserve direct route refresh.
- Use each page's own accurate meta title/description, `lang="en-MY"`, one H1, semantic landmarks, image alt and creative-portfolio disclaimers.
