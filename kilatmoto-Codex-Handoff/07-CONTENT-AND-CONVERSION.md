# Brand voice, credible copy and ISMAIL funnel

## Voice
Precise, energetic, premium, understated; Malaysian English with familiarity but without forced slang, jargon stacking or fake claims. Avoid generic superlatives (`Malaysia's No.1`, `award-winning`, `industry-leading`), warranties, customer counts and manufactured trust indicators.

## Sample copy (conceptual draft only)
- Brand lockup: `KILAT MOTORWORKS`.
- Eyebrow: `AUTOMOTIVE DETAILING / CONCEPT STUDIO`.
- H1: `PRECISION IN EVERY FINISH.`
- Standfirst: `A fictional Malaysian automotive care studio exploring the details that make every drive feel considered.`
- Service label: `Signature Exterior Detail`, etc. Protect the **exact six current names**.
- Project intro: `Six imagined automotive finishes. One obsession with the details.`
- Genuine ISMAIL invitation: `Own an automotive business? Explore what a premium website could look like for yours.` → verified ISMAIL link.
- Safety demo: `This is an interactive concept only. No message is sent, booked or stored.`

## Priority calls to action
1. For general automotive visitor persona: `Explore services` → `/services/`; `View project concepts` → `/projects/`.
2. For actual prospective business owner: `Website by ISMAIL` and descriptive case-study CTA → genuine ISMAIL site.
3. Contextual secondary CTAs (Story/Services/Projects) link to a concise creator presentation, without interrupting the fictional restaurant-style browsing experience.

## Conversion pathway
Search/portfolio referral → cinematic and transparent Home → six clear services and accessible project examples → user understands ISMAIL made the site → genuine creator CTA opens ISMAIL for real enquiries. Do **not** generate an actual car-care lead form, real price quotes or fake booking.

## Why this can appeal to Malaysian SMEs (hypothesis, not market proof)
Owners need fast mobile credibility, understandable service lists, visible before/after examples that are **real** when commercially offered, and a trustworthy contact path. This fictional demonstration is for showcasing capability; do not infer measured willingness to pay, revenue, conversion improvements or search results from the design alone. Validate commercial claims separately with real clients.

## Editorial/UX rules
- Tell the truth above the fold and on `/contact/`.
- Real `ISMAIL` destination must be visibly different from any non-sending demo.
- Internal links use genuine route destinations, not `href="#"` placeholders.
- Use actual six services and project concept labels as content source, not texts appearing on generated mockups.
- Avoid presenting AI-generated vehicle images as real customer proof; provenance in accessible disclosures.
