A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Better decisions begin with understanding
what your vehicle actually needs.

How is detailing different from a regular wash?+

A wash removes everyday dirt. Detailing takes a closer look at individual surfaces, contamination and finishing. The appropriate scope depends on the condition of the vehicle.

Can paint correction remove every scratch?+

No. Some marks sit too deeply to be safely polished out. Assessment and a suitable test area come first; preserving the finish matters more than chasing every mark.

Does ceramic protection mean less maintenance?+

A properly prepared coating can make some cleaning easier. It is not scratch-proof or maintenance-free, and still needs gentle washing and suitable aftercare.
