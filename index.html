A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Is KILAT MOTORWORKS a real workshop?

No. This is a fictional automotive detailing concept by ISMAIL. Services, imagery and projects illustrate a website experience; there are no real appointments or quotations.

Can paint correction remove every scratch?

No. Deep marks or thin clear coat can make removal unsafe. Assessment, a suitable test area and preserving the finish come first.

Is ceramic protection maintenance-free?

No. A coating is not scratch-proof and still needs gentle washing and suitable aftercare.

Does the enquiry demo send or save anything?

No. Validation takes place in this tab. No enquiry is sent, submitted, booked or saved. Use sample details only.
